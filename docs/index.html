1. Introduction

Welcome to Bhakti - Devotional Music ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy while providing a peaceful, sacred devotional music and spiritual audio listening experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you download and use our mobile application from the Google Play Store. By installing and using the App, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We respect the sanctity and privacy of your devotional practice:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, sign in with Google or social networks, or provide phone numbers or passwords to listen to devotional music.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect or store your real name, home address, contact details, payment information, or financial data.
  • No Audio or Voice Recordings: We do not record, store, or transmit your voice or background sound.

3. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

A. Stored Locally on Your Device

To deliver a personalized, seamless devotional experience without requiring a backend account, the App saves the following data strictly locally on your device storage:

  • Favorites & Playlists: Stotras, hymns, and bhajans you bookmark.
  • Playback Preferences: Playback speed (e.g., 0.75x, 1.0x, 1.5x), sleep timer duration, and repeat/shuffle states.
  • Language Choice: Your preferred UI and devotional lyrics language (Kannada, English, Hindi, Tamil, or Malayalam).
  • Audio Cache: Devotional songs and album artwork cached on your device to support offline listening and reduce network bandwidth consumption.

Note: This data never leaves your device and is erased if you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

B. Anonymous Diagnostics & Usage Analytics

We use Google Firebase services to monitor technical performance, app crashes, and aggregate app metrics (such as which hymns are completed or playback errors). All analytics data is aggregated and anonymous, cannot be used to identify individual users, and is used solely to enhance streaming stability and fix software bugs.

5. Device Permissions Requested

The App requests only the minimum set of permissions necessary to function properly:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to stream devotional music tracks, load spiritual lyrics, and fetch content updates from cloud storage.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required to maintain uninterrupted devotional playback when your phone screen turns off, when using other apps, or when managing playback via lock-screen notification controls.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Prevents the operating system from abruptly cutting off audio in the middle of long prayers, stotras, or meditation sessions.
  • RECORD_AUDIO (Optional): Only requested if you explicitly choose to use interactive chanting or audio visualizer features. Audio input is analyzed entirely in-memory on your device in real-time and is never recorded, saved, or uploaded to any server.
  • Photo & Video Permissions: We comply with Google Play's Photo and Video Permissions policy. End users are not asked for broad media storage permissions. Image selection (for administrative content upload) uses Android's modern system Photo Picker.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application contains sacred spiritual and devotional content suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take immediate steps to remove it.

7. Data Security and Retention

We value your trust. All network communications with cloud servers for music and metadata use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption. Since we do not maintain user accounts or personal profiles on remote servers, we do not retain your personal data.

8. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete Local Data: You can wipe all favorites, history, and cached songs at any time by clearing the App's storage via Android Settings > Apps > Bhakti > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Cache, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Advertising ID Reset: You can reset your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Inquiries: If you have any questions regarding your data or rights, you may contact the developer directly at the email below.
