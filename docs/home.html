Authentic Vedic Calendar

The Five Limbs of Hindu Panchanga

Panchanga literally means "Five Limbs" (Pancha-Anga) of time. The Bhakti app includes a built-in mathematical Vedic ephemeris engine that calculates your daily calendar details without relying on third-party tracking.

॥ ತಿಥಿರ್ವಾರಶ್ಚ ನಕ್ಷತ್ರಂ ಯೋಗಃ ಕರಣಮೇವ ಚ ॥

Traditional Hindu wisdom suggests understanding these five elements to harmonize daily spiritual practices, fasting, and prayers.

Tithi (ತಿಥಿ)
Lunar Day

Calculates the exact lunar phase from Pratipada to Purnima/Amavasya, indicating Shukla (waxing) or Krishna (waning) Paksha.

Vara (ವಾರ)
Weekday

The solar day of the week, connecting each day to its governing planetary influence and dedicated presiding deity (Deity of the Day).

Nakshatra (ನಕ್ಷತ್ರ)
Lunar Mansion

The Moon's position across the 27 Vedic constellations (from Ashwini to Revati) based on Chitrapaksha (Lahiri) Ayanamsha.

Yoga (ಯೋಗ)
Luni-Solar Angle

The 27 mathematical angular relationships between the Sun and Moon, signifying auspicious energetic periods.

Karana (ಕರಣ)
Half Lunar Day

Half of a Tithi (6 degrees of lunar separation), indicating favorable moments for initiating specific sacred activities.

Bhakti is an Android application designed as an all-in-one daily companion for Hindu spiritual living. It brings together high-quality devotional music, sacred stotras, and an authentic Vedic Panchanga calendar in a simple, elder-friendly, and distraction-free design.

Yes. The app features curated sacred chants and stotras (including Sri Vishnu Sahasranamam and Sri Lalitha Sahasranamam), bhajans, and mantras. It supports continuous background playback with notification controls, an A-B stanza looper for learning shlokas, sleep timers, and offline downloading.

The Hindu Panchanga is a traditional Vedic lunisolar calendar that marks time based on astronomical movements of the Sun and Moon. "Panchanga" refers to its five fundamental limbs: Tithi (lunar phase), Vara (weekday), Nakshatra (constellation), Yoga (luni-solar relationship), and Karana (half-tithi).

Bhakti provides daily Tithi (Shukla/Krishna Paksha), Vara (Day of the Week & Deity of the Day), Nakshatra, Yoga, and Karana. It also displays auspicious timings (Brahma Muhurtha and Abhijit Muhurtha), inauspicious intervals (Rahu Kalam, Yama Gandam, and Gulika Kalam), astronomical Sunrise, Sunset, Moonrise times, upcoming festival countdowns, and an overview monthly Vedic calendar.

The application interface and menus support 6 languages: Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ), Telugu (తెలుగు), English, Hindi (हिन्दी), Tamil (தமிழ்), and Malayalam (മലയാളം). You can change your preferred language at any time from the in-app settings.
