1. Introduction

Welcome to Bhakti - Devotional Music & Hindu Panchanga ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Srivatsa Malali. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the processing, local storage, and protection of information when you install and use our Android application distributed via the Google Play Store.

By installing and using Bhakti, you acknowledge the terms described in this policy. If you have questions regarding this document or our privacy practices, you may contact the developer directly at the contact details provided in Section 10.

2. Account Access and Personal Information

We respect the sacred nature of your devotional time:

  • No Account or Sign-In Required: End users are never required to register, create an account, sign in with Google or social networks, or provide phone numbers, email addresses, or passwords. All devotional music playback and Panchanga features are immediately accessible upon installation.
  • Administrative Login Only: The application contains an internal authentication gateway utilized solely by the developer to manage and curate catalog metadata. General devotees do not have, nor do they need, user accounts.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII) Collected: We do not ask for, collect, or store personal identity data such as your legal name, physical address, contacts list, or financial credentials.

3. Information Handled by the App

A. Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a tailored devotional experience without requiring a central user account or cloud user profile, the App stores preferences locally on your device:

  • Favorites and Playlists: Bookmarks of your favorite stotras, bhajans, and chants.
  • Playback Preferences: Audio playback speed, repeat mode, sleep timer configuration, and A-B stanza looping boundaries.
  • Language Selection: Your chosen interface and Panchanga language preference (Kannada, Telugu, English, Hindi, Tamil, or Malayalam).
  • Local Media Cache: Downloaded or cached devotional audio files and temple artwork stored locally on your device storage to enable offline listening and minimize data usage.

Note: This data is stored strictly on your local device and is removed when you clear the App's storage via Android Settings or uninstall the application.

B. Technical Analytics and Performance Telemetry

We use Google Firebase Analytics to measure aggregate application usage, evaluate feature adoption, and improve streaming stability. Analytics collection helps us understand:

  • Aggregate interaction events (such as songs started, songs completed, language selected, and search queries within the app).
  • Basic technical and diagnostic information (such as operating system version, device model, and general geographic region at country/city level).
  • Pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Firebase App Instance ID generated upon installation).

All analytics telemetry is processed in aggregate and is not linked to personal identifying information. Firebase Crashlytics is not currently integrated into the application.

4. Advertising Status & SDK Disclosure

Current Status — Free & Ad-Free: Bhakti is currently completely free to use and ad-free. Users do not need a subscription, paid plan, or payment to access existing features.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) Integration: The Google Mobile Ads SDK is present within the application codebase for planned future monetization after an initial ad-free period. In the current release:

  • Advertising is disabled via an internal feature configuration (adsFeatureFlag = 'N').
  • No banner, interstitial, rewarded, or native advertisements are displayed.
  • No ad requests are transmitted to Google AdMob servers during this initial ad-free period.
  • There is no automated or remote date-trigger that will activate advertisements without an intentional update.

If and when advertisements are introduced in a future release, they will be strictly family-friendly and contextual, and this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the active ad request behavior and consent mechanisms before ads are served.

6. Device Permissions Requested

Bhakti requests only the permissions necessary to deliver its core audio, Panchanga, and reminder features:

  • android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to stream devotional music, fetch sacred lyrics, verify connectivity status, and retrieve catalog updates from cloud storage.
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required to maintain uninterrupted devotional playback when your screen turns off, when you navigate to other apps, or when controlling playback via the lock-screen media notification.
  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: Ensures the device CPU does not prematurely terminate audio during lengthy stotra recitations, meditation sessions, or continuous chanting.
  • android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO (Voluntary Voice Search / Bhakti AI): Requested ONLY if you explicitly choose to tap the microphone icon to search for songs or ask devotional queries via voice. Speech recognition is processed transiently via Android's built-in speech recognition framework. Your voice is never recorded to an audio file, stored on device disk, or uploaded to our servers.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to display daily sacred shloka notifications, festival reminders, and media playback control notifications. You can enable or disable notifications at any time in Android Settings.
  • Photo & Media Selection: Bhakti complies with Google Play's Photo and Video Permissions policy. End users are not asked for broad media storage permissions (such as READ_MEDIA_IMAGES). Administrative image selection uses Android's modern system Photo Picker.

7. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Bhakti contains sacred spiritual and cultural content appropriate for families and audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in applicable jurisdictions). If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has provided us with personal information, please notify us and we will promptly delete it.

8. Data Security and Retention

All communication between the App and cloud services uses industry-standard HTTPS/TLS transport layer encryption. Because regular users do not create profiles or store personal identifying data on our backend servers, we do not retain personal user records. Local device preferences remain on your device until cleared or until the app is uninstalled.

9. User Rights and Data Controls

You have direct control over data stored on your device:

  • Clearing Local Data: You can erase all locally stored favorites, playback preferences, cached songs, and downloaded tracks at any time by going to Android Settings > Apps > Bhakti > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Cache.
  • Permissions Management: You can revoke optional permissions (such as Microphone for voice search or Notifications) at any time through your Android device settings.
  • Advertising ID Controls: You can reset or delete your device's Google Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Inquiries: If you have questions regarding data handling or privacy rights, you may contact the developer at the email address below.
